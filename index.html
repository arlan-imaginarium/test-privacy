Privacy policy: Ad Engine

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Ad Engine is an internal tool used by its owner to prepare and manage advertisements in the owner's own Meta ad account. It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no login with Facebook, and no way for members of the public to use it.

What the app does

The app uses Meta's Marketing API to create and read advertising objects (campaigns, ad sets, creatives and ads) in ad accounts and Pages that the owner controls. Ads are created in a paused state and are turned on only by a person in Meta Ads Manager.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about members of the public. The app does not read or store the personal data of Facebook or Instagram users. The only information it handles is advertising content written by the owner and the identifiers and settings of the owner's own ad account, Page and ads.

How information is used and shared

Advertising content and account identifiers are used only to create and manage the owner's ads through Meta's Marketing API. They are not shared with third parties other than Meta, as needed to run those ads.

Data deletion

The app stores no personal data about individuals. If you believe the app holds information about you and want it deleted, email arlanabante.business@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.
